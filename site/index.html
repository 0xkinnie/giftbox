GiftBox
GIFTBOX · A PAYBOX PLUGIN · BASE + ROBINHOOD CHAIN

Crypto gifts for people who don't do crypto.

GiftBox lets an AI agent send real crypto as a gift. Pick a token from a verified catalog, and the recipient gets a gift card with a claim code. They claim it into their own MoonPay wallet and can cash out to a bank account with MoonPay Sell.

Watch the demo on X Get PayBox

How it works

  1. 1

    Ask your agent

    Name the recipient and the gift. One sentence is enough.

  2. 2

    Pick a verified token

    USDC, USDG, cbBTC, ETH, or a tokenized stock from the catalog below.

  3. 3

    They get a gift card

    GiftBox creates the gift, an e-card image, and a claim code.

  4. 4

    They claim it

    The recipient opens a free MoonPay account to get a wallet address, then shares the address and the code.

  5. 5

    You approve

    PayBox shows you the transfer and you approve with your passkey. The crypto lands in their wallet, in their own custody.

Every contract was checked against chain explorers and CoinGecko's tracked-token list. A supply-cloned fake NVIDIA token circulates on Robinhood Chain. GiftBox lists only the real contract, so a clone can never be gifted.

Custody, plainly

  • NOTHING IS LOCKED UP
  • NO FUNDS MOVE UNTIL THEY CLAIM
  • CANCEL ANYTIME BEFORE A CLAIM

GiftBox holds no funds and no keys. The plugin runs fully offline and prepares unsigned proposals. PayBox executes every payment from the sender's wallet after passkey approval.
