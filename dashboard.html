🎁 GiftBox — demo

Send a real crypto token to someone with no wallet and no crypto experience. Pick from a curated catalog — stablecoins, BTC, ETH, tokenized stocks — they claim it with a code, get a free MoonPay wallet, and can cash out to their bank account with MoonPay Sell. No funds move until the gift is claimed.

1 · Sender — create a gift

Calls create_gift. Tokens resolve from GiftBox's verified catalog (contract addresses checked against the chain explorers and CoinGecko) — an agent can never gift a scam clone. Try gifting 0.5 NVDA on Robinhood Chain.

2 · Recipient — claim it

Calls check_gift (then prepare_claim in PayBox — an unsigned payment the sender approves with their passkey). Try the code from your gift card; a wrong code is rejected.

Demo of the GiftBox PayBox plugin (create_gift · create_ecard · check_gift · prepare_claim · mark_claimed · cancel_gift). The plugin never holds funds or keys: PayBox executes the claim payment from the sender's wallet after passkey approval. Cash-out via MoonPay Sell (moonpay.com/sell). Catalog: USDC/USDG · cbBTC (Base) · native ETH · Coinbase B20 stocks on Base (8 decimals) · Robinhood Token stocks on Robinhood Chain (18 decimals), all addresses verified 2026-10-17.
